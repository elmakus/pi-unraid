## Finding

The implementation does not provide the required serialized single-writer promotion boundary.

`promote()` performs two registry digest readbacks and then executes `docker buildx imagetools create`, but it holds no inter-process/distributed lock or other compare-and-swap primitive across that read-check-write sequence. Two promotion writers can therefore both observe the same expected digest on both pre-write readbacks and then concurrently write different candidate digests. A stale/superseded writer can mutate the alias before the final readback detects a mismatch.

The focused `test_newer_candidate_race_fails_closed` covers only a digest change between the first and second mocked readback. It does not exercise two concurrent writers crossing the second-read/write boundary, so the claimed stale/newer-candidate race protection and single-writer behavior are stronger than the implementation proves.

This violates the Card acceptance requiring a project-owned serialized promotion writer with stale/newer-candidate attempts failing closed, PUD-REQ-019's serialized mutable accepted channel, and P3's mutable-tag race control.
